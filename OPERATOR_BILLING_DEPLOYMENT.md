# Operator billing release

Do not deploy this API/web build until production migrations have been applied and validated.

1. Back up the production database and review its migration status. Apply the unchanged `042_provider_pricing_and_subscriptions.sql` and `043_monthly_settlements_and_fee_refunds.sql` if pending, then `044_verified_operator_commerce.sql` using the repository migration runner with the production database configuration. Never edit the checksum of an applied migration.
2. Run `pnpm db:status` and `pnpm db:validate` against that database with the release checkout and production environment. Require a current schema, matching checksums, all new tables/columns, and successful validation before deploying schema-dependent code. The monthly cron also refuses an unvalidated schema.
3. Review historic cash settlements that are reserved or already paid. Migration 044 marks unsent cash as directly received and backfills real walk-up platform fee debts from recorded cash payments. Reserved or paid historic cash requires finance review: never send its fare again. Confirm any fees already remitted against bank/cash evidence and record their collection through the finance CRM.
4. Review historic refunds and settlements. Existing confirmed fare refunds are carried into the new fare allocation field. Preserve provider-confirmed actual amounts; reconcile unknown partial allocations before releasing settlement funds. Do not infer that service/provider fees were retained.
5. Old submitted subscription references and `operator_plans` paid dates do not prove provider payment and do not grant new paid access. Operators confirm their plan and billing contact in the new checkout. No subscription may be charged before **2027-05-01 00:00 Africa/Porto-Novo** (2027-04-30 23:00 UTC). During the free period, selection records a preference only.

Configure FedaPay collections with `PAYMENT_PROVIDER=fedapay`, `FEDAPAY_ENVIRONMENT=live`, `FEDAPAY_SECRET_KEY`, and `FEDAPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` on the API project, and register the callback described in `.env.example` with FedaPay. Keep secrets server-side. Use the provider's hosted checkout to expose the methods and fees actually available on the merchant account. Do not configure an invented recurring mandate or retain card/Mobile Money credentials. Every renewal requires affirmative payment and server-side verification.

`FEDAPAY_PAYOUT_SECRET_KEY` is a separate payout credential and is not evidence that the merchant has FedaPay Payouts. Availability requires a provider-confirmed completed transfer recorded for the same account key fingerprint and environment. Sandbox evidence cannot enable live payouts. Without that proof, the monthly job prepares a manual reconciliation and never initiates a provider transfer. Establish live capability through a controlled provider pilot and reconcile the corresponding persisted request; do not fabricate a paid record to turn availability on.

Operators must opt in again with consent version `monthly-v1`; prior consent rows default to `legacy` and are excluded. The protected monthly cron needs `CRON_SECRET` and runs for the previous Benin calendar month. It excludes directly collected cash, offsets confirmed refunded operator fare, and reserves funds once per operator/month. A network timeout leaves a transfer reserved and pending reconciliation. Never retry an ambiguous provider transfer. A confirmed failed transfer can be reconciled to an independently verified manual bank settlement, with the exact paid amount, reference and evidence recorded by finance staff. Manual transfers never establish provider capability.
